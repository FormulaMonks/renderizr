What to react to

  • Two kinds. A notice (blue edge) says what the page did for the reader and leaves on its own after 6 seconds; hovering or focusing it holds it. An error (red edge) stays until the next set loads or someone closes it, and the page keeps the last set that loaded underneath.
  • Who sees what. The two reader messages show in built sites too; the contributor messages show only in dev; the author messages only in edit mode.
  • The held-edits question needs an answer before anything else happens. As a toast it can be missed; today's bar sits over the canvas. Does it move?
  • Stacking. A new message of the same kind replaces the old one, as today's bar does with its data-* kind.
